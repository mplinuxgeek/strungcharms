Click a bead to add it where the cursor is in the name box, or drag it onto the cord in the preview. It shows up in the name as a word in brackets, like [puck]. A barrel prints standing up, with the cord through its length.
Advanced: spacer bead sizes
Click a dot to set a colour. The number beside each colour is its filament slot: load that colour in that slot of your printer (AMS slot 1, 2, 3…). Click a bead in the preview to give that one bead its own colours; a new colour takes the next free slot.
One filament only? Choose Engraved under Letter and set every slot to 1. Beads of another colour then go on their own plate, so you can change filament between plates.

    Help

    How it works

    1. Type a name in the box at the top. Each letter becomes a bead.
    2. Go through the tabs: bead shape and size, the cord you will use, letter font, spacer beads, colours.
    3. Under Printer & export, pick your printer and slicer, then press Export 3MF.
    4. Open the downloaded file in your slicer, load the filament colours, slice and print.
    5. Thread the beads on the cord and knot both ends.
